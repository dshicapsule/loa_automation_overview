Interactive People Ops Reference

What This System Does

▾

When an employee submits their leave request through the LOA questionnaire, the system automatically handles everything: writing to the tracker, pulling their pay data, computing their pay schedule, emailing them a personalized leave plan, and queuing their Capsule top-up payments for payroll. People Ops doesn't need to manually trigger anything — the submission kicks it all off.

Three tabs work together

1. LOA Master2. Payroll Tracker3. Payroll Template
Central tracker. Every submission lands here. All leave details, status fields, and admin controls live in this tab. Auto-populated list of every Capsule top-up payment owed, one row per pay period, ready for payroll processing. Live-filtered view that shows only the payments due on today's payroll run — nothing else.

Step 1 — Employee Submits the Questionnaire

▾

The employee fills out the LOA questionnaire on Google Sites. It captures their leave type, state, leave start date, and whether they're full-time or part-time. On submission, the script runs automatically in the background.

What happens instantly

  • A new row is added to the LOA Master with all their leave details
  • The system looks up their pay data from the Census tab (base rate, pay type, work state)
  • A personalized leave confirmation email is sent to their personal email address
  • A heads-up email is sent to the employee's manager, with the employee cc'ed, notifying them of the upcoming leave
  • For full-time parental leave: a pay chart PDF is generated and attached to that email
  • For salaried FT parental leave: their Capsule top-up payments are automatically added to the Payroll Tracker
Part-time employees: PT employees receive a confirmation email noting they are not eligible for Capsule's paid parental leave benefit or Sun Life STD. No pay chart is generated and nothing is added to the Payroll Tracker.

Step 2 — The Pay Chart

▾

Full-time parental leave employees receive a personalized pay chart showing how their pay will be covered throughout their leave. The chart format depends on pay type:

Hourly EmployeesSalaried Employees
Weekly Leave Pay Chart — one row per week of leave, showing Sun Life, state PFL, and Capsule top-up amounts. Semi-Monthly Pay Chart — one row per Capsule payroll period (1st–15th and 16th–EOM), showing amounts prorated by working days on leave.

How the Semi-Monthly Pay Chart Is Calculated

For each semi-monthly pay period, the chart calculates the prorated gross pay based on how many working days the employee is on leave in that period. Order of calculation:

  • Sun Life STD pays first (Elimination and Disability phases)
  • State disability and state PFL pay next
  • Capsule tops up whatever remains to bring the employee to 100% of prorated pay

The % Pay column shows 100% during covered phases (Elimination, Disability, Capsule Bonding). State Extension periods show $0 from Capsule — the employee relies on state PFL only during those weeks.

Partial first period: If leave starts mid-period (e.g., the 5th), the first row is prorated. If they start on the 5th with 9 working days on leave out of 11 in that period, Capsule pay is calculated on 9/11 of their semi-monthly gross.

Step 4 — Running Payroll (Payroll Template)

▾

The Payroll Template tab is a live filtered view. It automatically shows only the Payroll Tracker rows due on the current payroll run.

Filter logic

  • Weekly employees: shows payments where Pay Date is 3 working days ago (Wed before a Friday payroll run)
  • Semi-monthly employees: shows payments where Pay Date is 4 working days from today
  • Only shows rows where col G (Processed) is blank

When a payment has been processed in Rippling, mark col G in the Payroll Tracker for that row. It disappears from the Payroll Template automatically.

Nothing showing? "No rows match today's payroll run" means no payments are due today. Check the Pay Date on relevant Payroll Tracker rows and confirm col G is blank for unprocessed rows.

Step 5 — Approval & Manager Notificationnew

▾

After a submission lands, People Ops reviews it in the LOA Master and records the approval. A separate agent then sends the manager the approval follow-up and creates the calendar invites.

People Ops approval steps (LOA Master)

  • Col AC — mark "Approved" once the leave is approved
  • Col AD — add verbiage to the intermittent-leave language if applicable
  • Col AE — check the box when you're ready to send the follow-up email to the manager, confirming the leave is approved
  • Col AG — auto-populates. This is the trigger the agent reads to send the manager notification and calendar invites; no manual entry needed.

The Agent

The agent runs automatically every day at 5:00 PM. To run it sooner, open the trigger and run it manually. After it runs, confirm:

  • An auto-reply is sent to the manager, via People Ops, confirming the leave is approved
  • Two calendar invites are created — one for the leave start date and one for the return-to-work date

Admin Controls & Manual Overrides

▾

The LOA Master has two admin checkboxes per row (far-right columns). Both show a confirmation dialog before running.

CheckboxWhat It Does
Col AM — Re-generate Payroll RowsDeletes any unprocessed Payroll Tracker rows for this employee and re-appends them fresh. Use when the leave schedule changed or original rows were incorrect.
Col AN — Re-generate Pay ChartRe-runs the pay calculation and re-sends the pay chart email to the employee. Use when the leave start date or other details were updated.

Updating a Leave Start Date

  • Update col I (Leave Start Date) directly in the LOA Master
  • Col J (Expected Return Date) recalculates automatically via its formula
  • Check col AN to re-generate and re-send the pay chart — confirmation dialog appears first
  • Check col AM to re-generate Payroll Tracker rows with the corrected dates

Census Data & Columns

▾

The Census tab is the source of truth for employee pay rates. If a base rate, pay type, or hours change, update Census first, then regenerate payroll rows via the col AM checkbox.

Key columns the system reads

  • Col C — Rippling Profile # (written to Payroll Tracker col A)
  • Col E — Full Name
  • Col O — Personal Email (used to match the employee)
  • Col Q — Base Rate (hourly rate or annual salary)
  • Col R — Pay Type: "Hour" = hourly, "Year" = salaried
  • Col W — Work State (determines state PFL/disability eligibility and rates)
  • Col Z — 8-week total hours (hourly only — divided by 8 for weekly average)

LOA Master Auto-Calculated Columns

Set automatically — do not overwrite manually:

  • Col J — Expected Return Date (leave start + total weeks × 7)
  • Col L — Total Leave Weeks (formula)
  • Col P — Base Weekly Wage (written at submission from Census)

Good to Know

▾
  • Pay data is read from Census at the time of submission. If Census is updated after submission, regenerate payroll rows (col AM checkbox) to pick up the new figures.
  • All pay chart amounts are estimates. Sun Life pays in a lump sum after claim approval. State PFL and disability pay separately on their own schedules.
  • If a pay period straddles the transition from Capsule Bonding to State Extension, it shows as Capsule Bonding (since most of the period is covered). The math accounts for this correctly.
  • State Extension periods intentionally show $0 Capsule pay — those weeks are unpaid by Capsule, job-protected, and funded only by state PFL.
  • New Payroll Tracker rows always land right after the last row with real data, even if column H has formulas dragged far down.
  • Before deleting any Payroll Tracker rows, check that col G is blank (unprocessed). Never delete rows already processed through payroll.

Recent pay-chart changes

  • Waiting-period week: the employee's Capsule paycheck shows 100% of pay during the 7-day waiting week, while state and Sun Life pay $0 that week. All chart formats now show this consistently.
  • Saturday-start leaves show a Week 0 pre-leave stub — no pay and no waiting period. The 7-day waiting period is Week 1, and benefits begin Week 2.
  • Sun Life STD pays 60% of weekly base, capped at $2,500 per week. State disability coordinates within that cap and Capsule tops up to 100%.
